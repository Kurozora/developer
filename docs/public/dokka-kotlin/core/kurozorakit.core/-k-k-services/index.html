class KKServices

KKServices is the object that serves as a provider for KurozoraKit services.

KKServices is used together with KurozoraKit to provide extra functionality such as storing sensitive information securely and showing success/error alerts. For further control over the information saved securely, you can provide your own KeyStore object with your specified properties.
